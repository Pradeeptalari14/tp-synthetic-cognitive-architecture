# Domain Terminology & Glossary — Synthetic Cognitive Architecture Studio

This glossary provides authoritative mathematical and algorithmic definitions for all concepts utilized across `tp-synthetic-cognitive-architecture`.

---

### Global Workspace Theory (GWT)
Cognitive architecture proposed by Bernard Baars where conscious cognition functions as a shared central blackboard coordinating distributed specialist modules.

### Variational Free Energy
Mathematical upper bound on informational surprise in Karl Friston's active inference framework, minimized through perception (updating beliefs) and action (altering sensory states).

### Salience Competition
Winner-take-all mathematical arbitration where sensory percepts bid for global broadcast based on L2 norm and Shannon entropy.
